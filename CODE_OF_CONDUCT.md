# Super Dolphin Agent Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Super Dolphin Agent welcomes contributors with different identities,
backgrounds, abilities, experience levels, and viewpoints. Members,
contributors, and maintainers commit to making participation safe,
professional, and free from harassment or exclusion.

We aim to build a community where people can question designs, report failures,
review AI-generated work, and disagree about technical choices without being
attacked or diminished as individuals.

## Our Standards

Behavior that strengthens the community includes:

- treating other people with patience, empathy, and respect;
- challenging code and ideas with evidence rather than attacking their authors;
- welcoming specific, constructive feedback and giving it in the same spirit;
- acknowledging mistakes, repairing their impact, and learning from them;
- making room for different levels of language fluency and technical
  experience; and
- choosing the long-term health of the project and community over personal
  advantage.

Behavior that is not acceptable includes:

- harassment, intimidation, discriminatory remarks, or sustained unwanted
  contact;
- sexualized language, imagery, attention, or advances;
- trolling, insults, threats, personal attacks, or deliberately humiliating
  another participant;
- exposing another person's private information without clear permission;
- retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith; and
- other conduct that would reasonably make a professional community unsafe or
  hostile.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting this Code of Conduct and
responding to violations consistently and proportionately. They may edit,
hide, reject, or remove comments, commits, code, issues, reviews, or other
contributions that conflict with these standards. They may also restrict a
participant's access to project spaces.

When privacy and safety permit, maintainers should explain moderation decisions
to the people directly involved. A maintainer with a personal conflict in a
report should not lead its review.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to repository activity, contribution review, and
every other space the project identifies as a Super Dolphin Agent community
space. It also applies when someone represents the project publicly, including
through an official project account, at an event, or while acting in an
appointed project role.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to the
repository owner through a private contact method currently published on the
[owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/lihah111222333-cloud). If the profile
does not provide a private contact method, and the canonical issue tracker is
already public, open a
[minimal conduct contact request](https://github.com/lihah111222333-cloud/super-dolphin-agent/issues/new?title=Private%20Code%20of%20Conduct%20contact%20request)
that asks only for a private reporting path. Do not include incident details,
personal data, screenshots, logs, or evidence in that public request. Wait for a
private path before sharing the report. Before publication, if neither a private
owner contact nor a public project tracker exists, retain the report privately;
the project does not claim an unavailable reporting channel.

Do not submit Code of Conduct reports through the security vulnerability
channel. Maintainers will handle reports as privately and impartially as the
circumstances allow, protect reporter information from unnecessary disclosure,
and consider the context, severity, pattern, and community impact before taking
action.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers use the following four-level framework to select a proportionate
response. These levels guide judgment; they do not require public disclosure of
a report or its evidence.

### 1. Correction

**Community impact:** An isolated use of inappropriate language or behavior that
is unprofessional or unwelcome but causes limited community harm.

**Response:** A private explanation of the problem and the expected correction.
The participant may be asked to edit or withdraw the material, acknowledge the
impact, or apologize to affected people.

### 2. Warning

**Community impact:** A repeated violation, a more substantial incident, or a
failure to make a previously requested correction.

**Response:** A written warning that identifies the prohibited behavior and the
consequences of continuing it. Maintainers may impose a defined period of no
contact with affected people or no participation in specified project spaces.
Breaking those conditions may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community impact:** Serious or sustained harmful behavior, including repeated
harassment, intimidation, or disregard for an earlier warning.

**Response:** Removal from project interaction and public communication for a
defined period. During the ban, the participant must not contact the people
involved in the enforcement action through project spaces or external channels.
Ignoring the ban may result in permanent exclusion.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community impact:** A severe incident or established pattern of harassment,
threats, aggression, discrimination, retaliation, or other conduct that makes
continued participation unsafe.

**Response:** Indefinite removal from project spaces and project-related public
interaction.
